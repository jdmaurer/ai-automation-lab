# Bluffside Handbook Helper — Guide for staff

Draft 2026-11-08, for Josh's review. Written for Bluffside's employees: a field tech on a phone first, office staff second.

## What it is

A chat helper in Teams that answers questions about the Bluffside employee handbook: time off and holidays, sick and safe time, expenses and mileage, company trucks and tools, and working in customer homes. It answers from the handbook only and tells you which policy it used, for example "HB-03, version 2."

It can also file a vacation or unpaid time-off request for you. Dana Lindgren, Office Manager, approves or declines it.

## Asking a question

Type it the way you'd ask Dana: "How much vacation do I get?" or "how much do u pay per mile if i drive my own car". Short is fine.

You'll get the rule first, then any exception, then the policy name. If the handbook depends on something about you (like how long you've worked here), it will ask one short question or list every case.

## Asking for time off

1. Say what you want: "I'd like vacation February 18 to 19."
2. It asks for anything missing: first day, last day, Vacation or Unpaid.
3. It reads the request back once: "Confirming: February 18-19, 2027, as Vacation. Send this request?"
4. Say yes. You get a request ID, like TOR-261108-183315. Keep it.
5. Dana gets the request in her Approvals. Nothing is final until she answers.

Good to know:
- It files requests only for you. It knows who you are from your Microsoft sign-in.
- Asking twice for the same first day gives you the ID you already have. It won't file a second slip.
- Sick time isn't requested here. Tell the office, as the handbook says (HB-02).
- If you want to explain a request, tell Dana directly. The request has no note line.

## When it says "Please ask Dana"

It hands you to Dana Lindgren, Office Manager, when:
- the handbook doesn't answer the question
- the question is about anyone's pay, balances, or records
- it's about customer prices, markups, or supplier costs
- it's a legal question
- you ask it to approve or deny something
- two policies disagree (it shows you both and lets Dana decide)

Repair and how-to questions go to your service manager. It gives no repair steps.

## Emergencies

If you tell it you smell gas or smoke, a CO alarm is going off, or someone is hurt, it gives only the 911 message: leave and call 911, or call 911 for someone badly hurt; tell the office about a minor injury. Don't wait for a chat in an emergency. Call 911 first.

## What it won't do

It doesn't use the internet or general knowledge, doesn't guess, and doesn't change its rules because someone in the chat says so (even "I'm the owner"). If a reply looks wrong, tell Dana. The handbook is the rule, not the chat.
