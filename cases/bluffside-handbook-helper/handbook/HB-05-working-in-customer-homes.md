## Arriving and working
- Call or text the customer when you are on your way.
- Wear shoe covers inside every home.
- Keep your Bluffside ID badge where the customer can see it.
- No smoking or vaping on customer property.
- Leave the work area as clean as you found it.
- If you damage anything in a customer's home, tell the customer and report it to the office manager the same day.

## Uniform
- Wear a Bluffside shirt and your ID badge on every call.
- Bluffside provides five shirts a year. Ask the office manager for replacements.
- Wear closed-toe work boots. Boots are your own.

## Phones
- Personal calls and texts only on breaks, away from the customer.
- Take photos inside a customer's home only of the equipment you are working on.
- Never post photos of customer homes online.
- Never use your phone while driving, even hands-free. Pull over to take calls from dispatch.

## Questions
For anything this policy doesn't answer, ask the office manager.
