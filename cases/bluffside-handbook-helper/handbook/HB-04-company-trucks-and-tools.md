## Using a company truck
- Company trucks are for work only.
- Techs on the on-call schedule may drive their truck home that week.
- No passengers except Bluffside employees, unless the office manager approves a ride-along.
- Phones only hands-free while driving.
- Fuel with the company card only.

## Weekly truck check
Every Monday before your first call, check tires, lights, fluids, and stock, and turn in the truck checklist.

## Accidents and damage
If anyone is hurt, call 911 first. Report any accident or damage to the truck to the office manager the same day, even if it's small.

## Tools
- Bluffside supplies specialty tools and meters. You supply your own basic hand tools.
- Tool allowance: Bluffside pays back up to $300 a year for hand tools, with receipts.
- Report lost or broken company tools to the office manager the same week.

## Questions
For anything this policy doesn't answer, ask Dana Lindgren, Office Manager.
