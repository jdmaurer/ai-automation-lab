## Paid holidays
Bluffside closes the shop on these days: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the Friday after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. Christmas Eve is a half day; the shop closes at noon.

Field techs on the holiday on-call schedule may be called out on a holiday. A tech who works a call on a paid holiday gets another full day off, taken within 30 days. Ask the office manager about holiday pay.

## Vacation
Full-time employees start earning vacation after 90 days of work.

| Years with Bluffside | Vacation per year |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 3 years | 1 week (40 hours) |
| 3 to 5 years | 2 weeks (80 hours) |
| 6 years or more | 3 weeks (120 hours) |

You may carry up to 40 unused vacation hours into the next calendar year. Hours over 40 are lost on January 1.

## How to request time off
Request vacation at least 2 weeks ahead, using the time-off request. The office manager approves or declines every request.

Busy seasons: field techs may not take vacation from December 15 to January 15 or from June 15 to August 15, the heating and cooling rush, unless the owner approves it.

## Unpaid time off
Unpaid time off is decided case by case. Ask the office manager.
