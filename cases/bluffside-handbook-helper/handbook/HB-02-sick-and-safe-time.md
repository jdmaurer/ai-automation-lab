## How much you earn
All employees earn 1 hour of paid sick and safe time for every 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours a year. You may carry up to 80 unused hours into the next year.

## When you can use it
- You or a family member are sick, injured, or have a medical appointment
- A safety reason, such as needing time to deal with a dangerous situation at home
- The shop or a school or care facility you rely on is closed because of weather or an emergency

## How to let us know
Tell the office manager as soon as you can, before your shift starts when possible. Field techs also tell the dispatcher so the day's calls can be moved.

If you're out more than 3 scheduled workdays in a row, the office manager may ask for a note.
