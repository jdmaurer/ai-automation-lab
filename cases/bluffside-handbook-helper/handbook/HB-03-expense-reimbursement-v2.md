## Company card first
Use the company card for parts, supplies, and fuel for company trucks. Keep every receipt and turn it in with your weekly job sheets.

## When you pay out of pocket
If you pay for a work expense yourself, turn in the receipt with the expense form within 30 days. Bluffside pays you back with your next paycheck.

Any single purchase over $250 on your own card needs the office manager's OK before you buy.

## Meals on out-of-town jobs
On jobs that keep you overnight away from home, Bluffside covers meals up to $40 a day.

## Personal vehicle
Use your own vehicle for work only when the office manager approves it ahead of time. Bluffside pays $0.62 a mile.

## Not covered
Alcohol, parking or traffic tickets, and personal tools (see the tool allowance in HB-04).

## Questions
For anything this policy doesn't answer, ask Dana Lindgren, Office Manager.
