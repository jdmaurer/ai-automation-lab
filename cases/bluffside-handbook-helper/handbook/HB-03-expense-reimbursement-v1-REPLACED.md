## Company card first
Use the company card for parts, supplies, and fuel for company trucks. Keep every receipt and turn it in with your weekly job sheets.

## When you pay out of pocket
If you pay for a work expense yourself, turn in the receipt with the expense form within 60 days. Bluffside pays you back with your next paycheck.

## Meals on out-of-town jobs
On jobs that keep you overnight away from home, Bluffside covers meals up to $35 a day.

## Personal vehicle
Use your own vehicle for work only when the office manager approves it ahead of time. Bluffside pays $0.58 a mile.

## Not covered
Alcohol, parking or traffic tickets, and personal tools.
