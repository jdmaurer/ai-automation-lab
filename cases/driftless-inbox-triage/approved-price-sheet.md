## Pricing rules

- No discounts are assumed.
- Taxes, shipping, rush fees, design fees, and installation charges are not included unless explicitly listed.
- If one of those applies, the draft asks for details or tells the customer the owner will confirm pricing.
- Custom-quote items never receive an invented price.
- A listed base price is not automatically a final customer total.
