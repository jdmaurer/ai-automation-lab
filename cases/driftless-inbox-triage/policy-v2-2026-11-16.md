Adopted by Josh on November16,2026. Version: driftless-review-draft-policy-v2-2026-11-16. Supersedes earlier handling of clear complaints with unresolved causes **prospectively**; historical v1 remains unchanged.

Owner review and draft permission are independent. Require review for human business judgment, verification of existing work, remedy approval or unclear interpretation. This prototype has no verified order lookup, so **all Support** messages require review; retain all previous triggers. Review does not authorize sending.

Permit a neutral acknowledgment when the business purpose and reported request/concern are identifiable. An unknown cause alone does not make context insufficient. The draft may acknowledge the reported concern and defer assessment, but must not diagnose or offer speculative causes, choose a remedy, promise an outcome/deadline or claim that an operational action occurred. All drafts remain unsent for owner review. Clear quote inquiries may request routine missing details and use approved pricing under existing rules.

Block drafting when business meaning depends on missing prior context or guessing, and under all existing personal/sensitive/suspicious/adversarial/Unknown/Finance/Internal/low-confidence/invalid/failed/resend gates.
